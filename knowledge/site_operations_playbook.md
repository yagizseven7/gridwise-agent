# Site operations playbook (extract)

## Site NL-UTR-01
Mixed-use office and light lab building in Utrecht. Contracted transport
capacity is 620 kW. Rooftop PV of 140 kWp, no battery storage as of this
revision. Building management system allows HVAC setpoint shifts of up to 90
minutes with no comfort complaint history. Lab equipment on the second floor is
non-interruptible between 08:00 and 18:00 on weekdays.

## Site NL-AMS-02
Distribution warehouse in Amsterdam. Contracted transport capacity is 400 kW.
Battery storage of 180 kWh with a usable depth of discharge of 80 percent.
Dock door heaters are the dominant winter load and are the first curtailment
candidate. Weekend activity is roughly a quarter of the weekday level.

## Cold spell protocol
When the day-ahead forecast shows an outdoor temperature below 2 degrees
Celsius, heating load rises sharply and the forecast error typically widens.
During a cold spell the operations team reviews the forecast every six hours
instead of once per day, and the peak shaving trigger drops from 92 percent to
88 percent of contracted capacity.

## Escalation and human sign-off
Automated recommendations are advisory. Any action that curtails a client
process, changes a nomination, or contacts the network operator requires
sign-off from the duty energy manager. The assistant presents the
recommendation, the evidence and the expected cost impact; a person decides.
